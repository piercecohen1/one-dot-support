Privacy Policy

Effective August 14, 2026

We do not collect your data

One Dot does not send us your mood entries, notes, usage, device information, or any other personal data. The app has no analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party software that collects data.

Storage and iCloud

Your mood history is stored locally on your devices. If you enable iCloud for One Dot, Apple syncs it between your devices through your private iCloud database. We do not have access to that database or its contents.

Exports and reminders

Exports are created on your device and go only to the destination you choose. Optional reminders are scheduled locally on your device.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to privacy@onedotmood.app.
